Five parts, each one a file in the repo.

Stored as codes, not audio

NanoCodec turns each note into 13 codebooks × 12.5 frames a second, packed 11 bits each into a .hct file. The strip at the top is the 60-second sample: 13,532 bytes.

nemo-nano-codec-22khz-1.78kbps-12.5fpsworker/worker.py

Deleted only after a check

The Job decodes the codes and transcribes them again. Only at 5 % word drift or less does the app delete the raw upload, after a 24-hour grace, and check it is gone.

nemotron-3.5-asr-streaming-0.6bhi-IN · 1.12 s

Leave with everything

Delete-all shows the listings come back empty. Export zips the codes, transcript and decode.py, which rebuilt the sample's audio without Hearth at 57.5 dB SNR.

What Hearth does not claim.

Returned to sender
  1. +4.09 pts

    The round trip is lossy. It adds +4.09 WER points on average; one normal-level clip got worse (13.0 % → 47.8 %). A note whose drift exceeds the gate keeps its original: 15 of the 20 clips were under the gate.

    Honest limits 4
  2. 6.6 min

    Time to kept is minutes, not seconds. Most of it is the Job starting and loading two models; the work itself is about a minute for a 60 s note.

    Honest limits 2
  3. 0 of 2

    CPU speed is ours. Neither NVIDIA card states CPU support; every speed number here was measured on cpu-d3.

    Honest limits 1
  4. 2 gates

    Nemotron 3 Super's card does not list Hindi. That is why the quote gate and field gate exist: its mistakes are dropped and counted, not shown.

    Honest limits 3
  5. 4 unused

    Deliberate refusals. NemoClaw, OpenShell, Hermes Agent and Tavily: the one flow needs no agent runtime and no live web.

Before you press play.

Is that a real family's voice?

No. Every voice on this page is FLEURS read speech, labelled where it plays. The consented, actor-voiced family sample replaces it once it is recorded.

— README · See it in Action

What happens to the raw audio?

It is kept 24 hours so you can compare, then deleted and checked. If the round trip changes more than 5 % of the words (or 30 % of any 8-word segment), the original stays, for you to delete.

— src/lib/pipeline.ts · the app's first screen

What does Token Factory see — and keep?

Transcript text only, never audio. It may keep prompts and outputs for Speculative Decoding (stored in Finland, never used for training) unless Zero Data Retention is confirmed; until then, spaces must opt in first.

— README · Honest limits 7

Why CPU — and why does a keep take minutes?

Both NVIDIA speech models run on cpu-d3: the ASR at a real-time factor of 0.16, the codec round trip at 0.63. Most of the ~6.6 minutes is the Job starting (≈164 s to a running container) and loading two models.
