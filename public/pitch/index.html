Hearth · v0.1.005 / 10
The browser puts audio straight into the bucket. The app starts a Serverless AI Job on CPU that runs NVIDIA's ASR and NanoCodec, and writes codes, transcript and report back. The app deletes the raw audio only if the round trip holds, after a 24-hour grace (or at once on “Delete original now”), and proves it with HEAD 404. Token Factory sees transcript text only.
